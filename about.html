About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery opened in 2015 as a small corner shop with one oven and a family sourdough starter. Today we bake for hundreds of neighbors each week, but every loaf is still shaped by hand.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We buy flour from regional mills, eggs and dairy from nearby farms, and fruit that is in season. Local ingredients mean fresher flavor and a stronger community.

Meet Our Staff

Head Baker

Our head baker starts the ovens at 3:00 a.m. and has tended our sourdough starter since day one.

Pastry Chef

Our pastry chef creates the croissants, danishes, and custom cakes our customers love.
